AURA LAB

20 procedural character auras for Blender

Turn any character into a power-up shot. Select a mesh, pick an aura, click Add. Every aura is built around its own geometric idea: fire tongues, lightning arcs, light-feather wings, a black hole, blood streams, soul wisps, a full-body dissolve. It follows animated rigs, renders in Cycles and Eevee, and needs no textures or UVs.

02 / 07  Workflow

PICK. CLICK. RENDER.

1

Install the zip

Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions > Install from Disk.

2

Select a mesh

Any character or object. Rigged, shape-keyed, sculpted. No UVs needed.

3

Pick an aura

Open the Aura Lab tab in the sidebar (N) and choose from the visual gallery.

4

Click Add

The aura is built around the mesh. Press Optimize Render Settings once per scene for AgX, volume quality, ray tracing and bloom.

Crimson Blood in the live Cycles viewport · Aura Lab sidebar on the right

03 / 07  Controls

EVERY LAYER IS YOURS TO TWEAK.

Each aura is a small stack of layers. The top sliders act on the whole aura; open a layer to reshape it. Everything is keyframable.

IntensityBrightness of every layer and light at once.
Hue Shift · SaturationRecolour the whole aura with one slider.
SpeedSlow it down for a dramatic beat or speed it up.
Start TimeTrigger the aura mid-shot, in seconds.
LayersToggle each layer for viewport and render, edit its colours, glow and every geometry input: count, length, spin, rise, flap, writhe...

Animate any value. In the showcase one parameter of each aura is keyframed live:

04 / 07  Animation

IT MOVES WITH THE BODY.

Aura Lab reads the evaluated mesh, so armatures, shape keys and modifiers all just work. Wings, the halo, tentacles, blood streams and soul wisps are anchored to patches of skin picked in the rest pose, so they stay on the back, the head and the torso while the character walks, runs or fights.

Stepping through the timeline in the Cycles viewport · Inferno on a Mixamo run cycle

RigsArmatures, Mixamo, Rigify, Auto-Rig Pro, anything that deforms the mesh
MeshesAny mesh, no UVs and no clean topology needed
RemoveRemove Aura deletes everything it added, including the dissolve modifier

05 / 07  Viewport

LOOK-DEV IT LIVE.

Every aura is procedural Geometry Nodes and shaders, so you see it straight in the viewport and change it while it plays. No baking, no simulation caches, no flipbooks.

06 / 07  Timing and colour

TRIGGER IT. TINT IT.

Effects that develop over time start at the aura's Start Time, so you can cut to them on a beat. Ashen Dissolve chars the body, crumbles it away and releases it as black fumes. Glacial grows its ice from the feet up, Titan Awakening splits the ground open.
