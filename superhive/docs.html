AURA LAB DOCS

How to install Aura Lab, add an aura to a character, tune it, time it and render it. The add-on lives in the Aura Lab tab of the 3D Viewport sidebar (N).

5.0+  Blender2  Cycles + Eevee0  dependencies

01 / 09  Guide

INSTALL, UPDATE, REMOVE

  1. Download aura_lab-2.3.0.zip from your Superhive downloads. Don't unzip it.
  2. In Blender open Edit › Preferences › Get Extensions, open the arrow menu at the top right, choose Install from Disk… and pick the zip. Dragging the zip onto Blender works too.
  3. In the 3D Viewport press N and open the Aura Lab tab.

Update: install the new zip the same way. Auras already in your files keep working.

Remove: select the aura in Edit and press Remove Aura. It deletes the aura object, its lights and, for Ashen Dissolve, the dissolve modifier on the character.

02 / 09  Guide

ADD AN AURA

1. Select the mesh: a character, a creature, a statue. It does not need UVs.

2. Click the large preview to open the gallery and pick one of the 20 auras. The name, category and a one-line description show under it.

3. Click Add to <mesh>. A new object Aura - <name> (<mesh>) is created; it reads the mesh live, so you never need to move or parent it.

4. Press Optimize Render Settings once per scene (see below).

You can add several auras to one mesh, or the same aura to several meshes. Edit picks the aura the panels below control.

03 / 09  Guide

CONTROLS

TargetThe mesh the aura reads. Change it to move the aura to another character.
IntensityScales the glow of every layer and the aura lights.
Hue ShiftRotates the colour of the whole aura (−0.5 to 0.5).
Saturation0 makes it grey, 1 is the preset colour, up to 2 for extra punch.
SpeedPlayback speed of all motion: flames, orbits, flapping, flicker.
Start TimeSeconds into the scene when the aura starts. Effects that grow (Glacial, Titan, Ashen) begin here.
Reset ControlsBack to the preset values.
Remove AuraDeletes the aura and everything it added.

04 / 09  Guide

LAYERS

Every aura is a stack of layers: one signature layer (wings, chains, tentacles, beams...) plus support layers such as a rim shell, a volume (fire, mist, smoke) and particles (embers, sparks, ash).

The two icons on each layer toggle it in the viewport and the render. Open a layer to edit its material values (colours, glow, flow) and all its geometry inputs (count, length, radius, spin, rise, flap, writhe...).

Every value is a normal Blender property: hover and press I to keyframe it, or right-click to add a driver.

05 / 09  Guide

RENDERING

Optimize Render SettingsSets AgX view transform, finer volume steps, ray tracing in Eevee (for ice and water refraction) and a bloom/glare compositor. Run it once per scene.
CyclesBest quality for volumes (fire, smoke, mist) and refraction. A GPU with OptiX or HIP is recommended.
EeveeFast previews and stylized renders. Turn on Raytracing for Glacial and Hydro.
Motion blurSupported. Auras are regular geometry and volumes, so motion blur, depth of field and view layers work as usual.
VolumesEach aura has at most one volume layer. If a volume looks blocky, lower its Voxel Size in Layers.

06 / 09  Guide

ANIMATED CHARACTERS

Auras read the target's evaluated mesh every frame, after armatures, shape keys and modifiers. Wings, halo, tentacles, blood streams and soul wisps are anchored to skin patches chosen in the rest pose: Aura Lab turns on the mesh's rest position attribute for this automatically.

Works withArmatures (Mixamo, Rigify, Auto-Rig Pro, custom), shape keys, deforming modifiers and Geometry Nodes
ScaleDesigned for human-sized characters (about 1.6–2 m). For very large or small meshes, adjust Radius, Length and Voxel Size in Layers.
Cloth and hairOnly the target mesh is read. Pick the body as the Target.

07 / 09  Guide

TIMING EFFECTS

Start Time (Controls) is when the aura begins, in seconds. Auras that develop over time also have duration values in their layers:

Ashen DissolveThe body chars, crumbles away and leaves as black fumes. It starts at Start Time and follows Speed. The body itself is hidden by an AL_Dissolve modifier that Aura Lab adds to the character (Remove Aura removes it).
GlacialIce grows out of the skin and frost creeps up from the feet over Grow Time.
Titan AwakeningThe ground splits open, then rock tears loose and rises.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Nothing appearsCheck the frame is after the aura's Start Time, and that the viewport is in Material Preview or Rendered mode.
Effects frozen or black after opening a fileAllow Auto Run Python Scripts for the file (Blender asks when it opens). Aura timing uses drivers.
Wings or tentacles in the wrong placeThe Target must be the body mesh. If the mesh was replaced, press Remove Aura and add it again.
Blocky smoke or fireLower Voxel Size of the volume layer, or raise volume quality in Render settings (Optimize Render Settings does this).
Slow viewportHide the volume layer in the viewport (its screen icon) while animating; it still renders.
Ice or water looks flat in EeveeTurn on Raytracing in Eevee render settings (Optimize Render Settings does this).
